# Table S1. Eligibility criteria formulated under the three-track PICOS framework

| PICOS component | Track 1: Preclinical murine challenge meta-analysis (k = 22 experiments from 20 publications; N = 574 mice) | Track 2: In silico MEVC quality appraisal (k = 22 studies) | Track 3: Regional ICU & hospital molecular epidemiology synthesis (k = 17 cohorts; 1,724 analyzed isolates/episodes) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Population (P)** | Laboratory mice (BALB/c, C57BL/6, or ICR/CD-1) challenged with lethal inocula of clinical MDR/CRAB or reference *Acinetobacter baumannii* strains via intraperitoneal, intranasal, or intratracheal routes. | *A. baumannii* whole proteomes, reference genomes (ATCC 19606, ATCC 17978), or clinical pan-genomic sequence datasets. | Hospitalized and ICU patients with *A. baumannii* infections and clinical isolates recovered across tertiary hospitals in Egypt (*k* = 12) and the wider MENA region (*k* = 5). |
| **Intervention (I)** | Active immunization with defined recombinant outer membrane protein (OMP) or surface protein subunits (e.g., OmpA, BamA/Oma87, OmpW, BauA, ZnuD, Omp22, FilF, NucAb, Blp1, Wza, YiaD, CAM87009.1), scaffolded loop hybrids (LCL), or recombinant multi-epitope constructs (rMEP, AMEV1/2/5). | Computational reverse vaccinology and multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) engineering integrating predicted B-cell, CTL, and HTL epitopes. | Phenotypic antimicrobial susceptibility surveillance (carbapenems and colistin), carbapenemase and polymyxin-resistance genotyping (*bla*OXA-23, *bla*NDM, *pmrCAB*), and molecular strain typing. |
| **Comparator (C)** | Concurrent mock-immunized control cohorts receiving adjuvant alone, vehicle buffer (PBS), or carrier control. | Structural biology, immunogenetic, and safety benchmarks codified in our 7-domain quality rubric (0–14 points; Section 2.5). | Disaggregation by sampling frame (strictly unselected surveillance vs. MDR/outbreak surveys vs. CRAB-preselected collections) and colistin AST method (reference ISO 20776-1 BMD vs. non-reference methods) interpreted against CLSI M100 (34th ed., 2024) and EUCAST (v14.0, 2024). |
| **Outcomes (O)** | Host survival (*e*~v~/*n*~v~ vs. *e*~c~/*n*~c~) at the study-defined post-challenge endpoint (4 to 30 days where stated; ≥ 7 days in 19/22 experiments), synthesized via DerSimonian–Laird RR (with HKSJ adjustment) and additive RD. Organ bacterial burden and passive serum transfer were recorded qualitatively where reported but not pooled. | Domain-specific and composite quality scores (0–14) across pan-genome scale, target clinical genomes, β-barrel extracellular-loop excision, multi-locus HLA typing, peptide-level human proteome screening, 3D modeling/TLR docking, and validation depth. | Isolate-weighted carbapenem resistance (%), reference BMD colistin resistance (%), *bla*OXA-23 and *bla*NDM prevalence, MLST/pulsotype distribution, and reported cohort all-cause mortality (with study-specific time windows). |
| **Study design (S)** | Controlled peer-reviewed *in vivo* animal intervention trials reporting exact survivor/total counts (or exact survival percentages/deaths paired with stated group sizes). Whole-cell bacterins, live-attenuated mutants, outer membrane vesicles (OMVs), unconjugated/glycoconjugate capsules, DNA/mRNA vaccines, passive-only studies, and graph-only survival reports were excluded (Table S2). | Primary peer-reviewed computational reverse vaccinology and MEVC design articles published between January 2018 and October 2026. | Prospective or retrospective clinical surveillance cohorts, resistance-mechanism investigations, and whole-genome sequencing studies from Egyptian and MENA hospitals. |
