### Table S1: Methodological Eligibility Criteria Formulated Under the Structured Tri-Track PICOS Framework

| PICOS Component | Track 1: Preclinical Murine Challenge Meta-Analysis ($k = 25$ Trials) | Track 2: *In Silico* MEVC Quality Appraisal ($k = 22$ Pipelines) | Track 3: Regional ICU Molecular Epidemiology Synthesis ($k = 17$ Cohorts) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Population (P)** | Laboratory mice (BALB/c, C57BL/6, or ICR/CD-1; male or female; immunocompetent, diabetic, or neutropenic) challenged with lethal inocula of clinical or reference *Acinetobacter baumannii* strains. | *Acinetobacter baumannii* whole proteomes, reference genomes (ATCC 19606, ATCC 17978), or contemporary clinical pan-genomic sequence databases. | Consecutive hospitalized patients with confirmed ICU-acquired *A. baumannii* infections, and non-duplicate clinical isolates recovered from ICU specimens across Egypt and the MENA region. |
| **Intervention (I)** | Active immunization with recombinant outer membrane protein (OMP) subunits (e.g., OmpA, BamA/Oma87, OmpW, BauA, Omp22, OmpK), chimeric loop fusion antigens, or passive transfer of hyperimmune murine/rabbit sera or monoclonal antibodies (mAbs). | Multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) engineering integrating computationally predicted cytotoxic T-lymphocyte (CTL), helper T-lymphocyte (HTL), and linear/conformational B-cell epitopes linked by flexible peptide bridges. | Clinical surveillance of antimicrobial susceptibility patterns, phenotypic resistance testing (carbapenems and colistin), carbapenemase and colistin resistance genotyping, and molecular clonal lineage determination. |
| **Comparator (C)** | Placebo- or mock-immunized control cohorts receiving vaccine formulation adjuvant alone (e.g., Alum, Freund's), vehicle buffer (phosphate-buffered saline; PBS), or non-immune normal control serum. | Benchmark bioinformatics standards, established immunoinformatic threshold criteria, and our objective 7-domain translational quality appraisal rubric. | Comparative diagnostic performance between automated cards (bioMérieux VITEK-2) and reference broth microdilution (BMD; ISO 20776-1) evaluated against CLSI M100 (2024) and EUCAST (v14.0, 2024) breakpoints. |
| **Outcomes (O)** | **Primary:** Host survival rate (%) at a predefined study endpoint ($\ge 7\text{--}14$ days post-lethal challenge).<br>**Secondary:** Quantitative organ bacterial clearance ($\Delta\log_{10}\ \text{CFU/g}$ reduction in pulmonary or systemic visceral tissues) and serum opsonophagocytic killing (OPK) activity. | Methodological compliance score across the objective 7-domain rubric (0–14 scale), including pan-genome breadth, regional strain inclusion, extracellular loop confinement, multi-locus HLA restriction, human autoantigen screening, 3D structural validation, and wet-lab translational depth. | Summary carbapenem resistance rate (%), true colistin resistance rate (%), detection of Very Major Errors (VME; false susceptibility), distribution of carbapenemase genotypes ($bla_{\text{OXA-23}}$, $bla_{\text{NDM-1}}$), and 30-day crude and attributable patient mortality. |
| **Study Design (S)** | Controlled *in vivo* laboratory animal intervention trials with concurrent, vehicle-controlled challenge arms. Studies evaluating whole-cell killed bacterins, live-attenuated mutants, outer membrane vesicles (OMVs), or non-protein conjugates were excluded from the quantitative subunit synthesis. | Original peer-reviewed computational reverse vaccinology and immunoinformatic design investigations published between January 2018 and October 2026. Narrative reviews and commentary articles without primary constructs were excluded. | Prospective or retrospective observational clinical surveillance cohorts, molecular epidemiology studies, and whole-genome sequencing investigations conducted in tertiary hospital ICUs. |
