# Table S1. Eligibility criteria formulated under the three-track PICOS framework

| PICOS component | Track 1: preclinical murine challenge meta-analysis (k = 25 trials) | Track 2: in silico MEVC quality appraisal (k = 22 studies) | Track 3: regional ICU molecular epidemiology synthesis (k = 17 cohorts) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Population (P) | Laboratory mice (BALB/c, C57BL/6, or ICR/CD-1; male or female; immunocompetent, diabetic, or neutropenic) challenged with lethal inocula of clinical or reference Acinetobacter baumannii strains. | A. baumannii whole proteomes, reference genomes (ATCC 19606, ATCC 17978), or contemporary clinical pan-genomic sequence databases. | Hospitalized patients with confirmed ICU-acquired A. baumannii infections, and non-duplicate clinical isolates recovered from ICU specimens in Egypt and the MENA region. |
| Intervention (I) | Active immunization with recombinant outer membrane protein (OMP) subunits (e.g., OmpA, BamA/Oma87, OmpW, BauA, Omp22, OmpK), chimeric loop fusion antigens, or passive transfer of hyperimmune murine or rabbit sera or monoclonal antibodies. | Multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) engineering that integrates computationally predicted CTL, HTL, and linear or conformational B-cell epitopes joined by flexible peptide linkers. | Clinical surveillance of antimicrobial susceptibility patterns, phenotypic resistance testing (carbapenems and colistin), carbapenemase and colistin resistance genotyping, and molecular clonal lineage determination. |
| Comparator (C) | Placebo- or mock-immunized controls receiving adjuvant alone (e.g., alum, Freund's adjuvant), vehicle buffer (PBS), or non-immune normal serum. | Benchmark bioinformatics standards, established immunoinformatic threshold criteria, and the 7-domain quality rubric used in this review. | Comparison of automated cards (bioMérieux VITEK-2) with reference broth microdilution (BMD; ISO 20776-1), interpreted against CLSI M100 (2024) and EUCAST (v14.0, 2024) breakpoints. |
| Outcomes (O) | Primary: host survival (%) at a predefined endpoint (7–14 days or longer after lethal challenge). Secondary: organ bacterial clearance (Δlog10 CFU/g in pulmonary or visceral tissue) and serum opsonophagocytic killing activity. | Methodological compliance score on the 7-domain rubric (0–14), covering pan-genome breadth, regional strain inclusion, extracellular loop confinement, multi-locus HLA restriction, human proteome autoantigen screening, 3D structural validation, and wet-lab validation depth. | Carbapenem resistance rate (%), colistin resistance rate (%), very major errors (false susceptibility), carbapenemase genotype distribution (blaOXA-23, blaNDM-1), and 30-day crude and attributable mortality. |
| Study design (S) | Controlled in vivo animal intervention trials with concurrent, vehicle-controlled challenge arms. Studies of whole-cell killed bacterins, live-attenuated mutants, outer membrane vesicles (OMVs), or non-protein conjugates were excluded from the quantitative subunit synthesis. | Original peer-reviewed computational reverse vaccinology and immunoinformatic design studies published between January 2018 and October 2026. Narrative reviews and commentaries without primary constructs were excluded. | Prospective or retrospective observational surveillance cohorts, molecular epidemiology studies, and whole-genome sequencing studies conducted in tertiary hospital ICUs. |
